Let $V$ be a separable Hilbert space equipped with inner product $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$ and induced norm. (If a more general Banach manifold is required, the Hilbert case is the primary working model; the generalization is a controlled extension under B7'.)

**Axiom T2 (Ground --- I AM)**  
There exists a unit vector $\Phi\in V$ fixed by the maximal unitary group that preserves the energy observable. $\Phi$ is the mode **I AM**.  

Theorem (Residual): any observable that would distinguish internal structure of $\Phi$ beyond this invariance has expectation value undefined or infinite; residual unobservability is therefore mandatory (B1).

**Axiom T3 (Will --- I WILL)**  
There exists a (possibly unbounded) self-adjoint operator $H$ generating a strongly continuous unitary group $U_t = e^{-itH}$ (Stone's theorem). The infinitesimal generator and its flow constitute the mode **I WILL**.

**Axiom T3' (Admissible Extension --- Open Sector)**  
The unitary sector of T3 is retained unmodified as the closed-system case. Admit in addition a dilation sector: $\Psi$ is a CPTP map on a subsystem of $V \otimes V_{\text{env}}$, unitary on the total space (Stinespring). This is required for consistency: the unitary group of T3 conserves von Neumann entropy, so a coherence functional $C(\rho) = -S(\rho)$ cannot strictly increase under it, which the Process Equation demands of $\Psi$. Strict increase of $C$ on the subsystem is consistent with unitarity on the whole.

Omnipotence (§1.2) is correspondingly defined against the full unitary group of $V$, not the one-parameter subgroup generated by a single $H$, which is not transitive on projective space.

T5 exhaustiveness is preserved: the environment sector is a factor of the same $V$, not a fourth substance.

**Axiom T4 (Have --- I HAVE)**  
Let $\Omega$ be any state that is a stable attractor of the dynamics in the weak topology (or in a chosen coherence norm derived from entanglement entropy). Each such $\Omega$ is a mode **I HAVE**.

**Axiom T5 (Trinitarian Unity)**  
The three modes jointly exhaust the substance:

$$
V \ni \text{Energy} \;=\; \mathrm{span}\{\Phi\} \;+\; \text{flows of } H \;+\; \text{attractors } \Omega

No additional independent substance exists.

**Definition (Process Equation)**

$$
\Lambda_t(\Phi) \;=\; \int_\gamma \frac{d}{ds}\bigl(\Phi \otimes \Psi(s)\bigr)\,ds \;=\; \Omega

is well-defined when $\Psi$ is a (possibly nonlinear) completely positive map that strictly increases a fixed coherence functional $C$ (e.g. $C(\rho) = -S(\rho)$ for density operators) and the integral converges in norm.

## 2. Anarchy-Ontology

A relativized formal language that distinguishes **anarchy** (structural facts about a ruled-by relation $R$) from **anarchism** (ascriptive attitudes toward a target relation).

The framework forces explicit indices on every claim and maintains a strict separation between the two layers. No theorem is asserted that bridges structural facts about $R$ to ascriptive facts about speakers or interpreters. It is offered as a clarifying instrument, not as a political program, a theological system, or a complete theory of power.

### Motivation, Scope, and Provisional Status

Most persistent disputes about anarchy and anarchism collapse two independent questions or suppress required indices. This document isolates those questions. Every structural claim must carry a relation-domain index and a node-subdomain index; unindexed claims are treated as ill-formed.

The framework is deliberately limited and provisional:

- It does not decide whether any historical figure "was an anarchist."
- It does not rank regimes normatively.
- It does not claim that formal distinctions dissolve substantive political or theological disagreement; they only relocate the disagreement to an explicit premise (most often a sortal assignment).

**Strongest external objection (acknowledged):** high-density formal treatment of anarchism, especially when paired with close analysis of early Christian source texts, currently attracts pathologizing dismissal in some reception environments regardless of internal rigor. This is treated as a social constraint on presentation, not as a reason to dilute the formal content.

### I. Structural Layer --- Anarchy

#### Core Definition

$$
\mathrm{An}_R(x) \iff \neg \exists y\ (y \neq x \land x\, R\, y)
$$

- $R$ is an irreflexive ruled-by relation on a finite set $S$.
- $x\, R\, y$ means "$x$ is ruled by $y$".

$$
A_R = \{ x \in S \mid \mathrm{An}_R(x) \}
$$

Self-rule is not counted as being ruled (*auto-nomos* is compatible with $\mathrm{An}_R$).

#### Two Predicates

- **Local:** $\mathrm{an\text{-}archy}_1(x) := \mathrm{An}_R(x)$
- **Systemic:** $\mathrm{An\text{-}archy}_2(S,R) := (\rho_M = 0)$ (every ruler is itself ruled)

#### Apex vs Hermit

$$
\begin{align*}
\text{apex} &\iff \mathrm{An}_R(x) \land \exists z\ (z\, R\, x) \\
\text{hermit} &\iff \mathrm{An}_R(x) \land \neg \exists z\ (z\, R\, x)
\end{align*}
$$

#### Relativization

Every claim requires two indices: relation domain $d$ and node subdomain $D \subseteq S$.

$$
\mathrm{An}^D_R(x) \iff x \in D \land \neg \exists y \in D\ (y \neq x \land x\, R\, y)
$$

$$
\rho^D_A = \frac{|A^D_R|}{|D|}, \qquad \rho^D_M = \frac{|A^{D,\mathrm{apex}}_R|}{|D|}
$$

Three practical subdomains:

- **Sortal:** $S = H \uplus T$ (humans vs non-human/transcendent nodes)
- **Communal:** $C \subseteq H$
- **Full:** $D = S$

**Theorem (Sortal Divergence)**  
$\rho^H_A$ and $\rho_M$ are independent. A unique $t \in T$ ruling all of $H$ yields:

$$
\rho^H_A = 1 \quad \text{while} \quad \rho_M = \frac{1}{|S|} > 0
$$

The same configuration is maximal anarchy under the human sort and pure monarchy under the unsorted reading. Disputes over "theocratic anarchism" reduce to this single divergence.

#### Key Ratios

$$
\begin{align*}
\rho_A &= \frac{|A_R|}{|S|} \\
\rho_M &= \frac{|A_R^{\mathrm{apex}}|}{|S|} \\
\beta &= \frac{|R|}{|S|} \\
\sigma &= \frac{|R|}{|\{ y \mid \exists x\ (x\, R\, y) \}|}
\end{align*}
$$

Identity: $\beta = \sigma \cdot \rho_{\mathrm{rul}}$.

#### Regimes

| Regime | Characterization |
|---------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| Concentration | Low $\rho_A$, high $\sigma$ |
| Universal | $A_R = S \iff R = \varnothing$ |
| Residual | $A_R \neq \varnothing$ while $R \neq \varnothing$ |
| Apexless | $\rho_M = 0$ with $\lvert R\rvert > 0$ |
| Tyranny | $\lvert A_R^{\mathrm{apex}}\rvert = 1$, minimal $\rho_A$ |
| Horizontal | $R \upharpoonright D = \varnothing$ with $R \neq \varnothing$ |

#### Core Results

- **Antitone:** $R \subseteq R' \implies A_{R'} \subseteq A_R$
- **Amplification:** Deleting $(a,b)$ enlarges $A_R$ iff $b$ was $a$'s unique ruler
- **Universality:** $A_R = S \iff R = \varnothing$
- **Restriction monotone:** Shrinking the subdomain can only create anarchy

**Theorem (Circularity)**  
If $\rho_M = 0$ and $R \neq \varnothing$, then $R$ contains a cycle.

**Corollary**  
Horizontal anarchy over $D$ does **not** imply apexlessness. The two notions pull in opposite directions.

**Aggregation Theorem**  
Any isomorphism-invariant measure that depends only on the set $A_R$ is a function of $\rho_A$ and $\lvert S\rvert$. Systemic anarchy cannot be recovered from personal anarchy alone.

In multi-agent AOAP settings the same holds: systemic ratios remain functions of the individual $A_R$ sets and cannot be recovered from label density or ascriptive claims alone.

**Corollary (Overlap Aggregation)**  
Any isomorphism-invariant measure of group coherence depending only on the set of pairwise overlaps $\{\omega(C_i, C_j)\}$ is a function of the overlap multiset and $n$ alone. Group coherence is therefore not recoverable from pairwise overlap: high mutual agreement across all pairs is consistent with total group incoherence, exactly as high $\rho_A$ is consistent with $\rho_M > 0$. Consequence for §9: an $n$-party `CO_INFER` gated on pairwise $\omega$ has no valid coherence claim.

#### Edge Rule (Stipulative)

$x\, R\, y$ holds when $y$ can impose a directive that $x$ cannot unilaterally refuse without sanction, and $y$ is an identifiable person, office, or (under explicit sortal extension) transcendent node. Above household scale, nodes are offices.

### II. Ascriptive Layer --- Anarchism

> "One of the problems with dealing with anarchism is that there are many people whose ideas are anarchist, but who do not necessarily call themselves anarchists."  
> --- Howard Zinn (2008)

#### Predicates

$$
\begin{align*}
L(x) &\iff x \text{ self-identifies as an anarchist} \\
I^k_j(x) &\iff \text{interpreter } j \text{ reads } x \text{ as advocating } R^* \text{ with property } k
\end{align*}
$$

#### Three Advocacy Types

- $I^1$: maximize $\rho_A$ (individualist)
- $I^2$: force $\rho_M = 0$ (acephalous)
- $I^3_D$: force $R \upharpoonright D = \varnothing$ (horizontal / sortal)

$I^3_H$ is satisfied by configurations that maximally violate $I^2$. The two targets are incompatible.

#### Key Points

- $I^k_j(x) \not\Rightarrow L(x)$. The label-free set is non-empty.
- The word "anarchist" is mid-19th century; all pre-1840 subjects have $L(x) = \bot$ by construction.
- Titles ($\mathrm{Ttl}$) are a third, independent object. Abolishing honorifics does not delete edges of $R$.
- Servant-inversion constrains conduct along an edge; it does not delete the edge.

No aggregation theorem connects the density of labels or interpretations to any structural ratio.

### III. Worked Example: Early Christian Source Texts

A single test case exercising every index. The formalism does not settle doctrinal questions; it isolates the precise point at which doctrine must be supplied.

#### Structural Readings

- **Political domain:** $\mathrm{An}(x) = \bot$
- **Religious domain (unsorted):** $\rho_M > 0$ (Matt 28:18, 19:28, 18:15--18)
- **Religious domain (sorted $H/T$):** $\rho^H_A = 1$, $\rho_M = 1/\lvert S\rvert$ (Matt 23:8--10)
- **Communal restriction:** horizontal claim inside $C$ (Mark 10:42--45)

Two internal tensions are resolved only by distinguishing titles from edges and conduct-along-edges from edge deletion. Under that resolution the material is not $I^2$.

#### The Hinge

$\rho^H_A = 1$ requires the relevant node to be placed in $T$. If placed in $H$, both ratios change immediately. The entire anarchism reading turns on this single sortal assignment --- a doctrinal commitment the formalism cannot supply.

Readers who assign $T$ and readers who assign $H$ are both reasoning correctly from their respective premises.

#### Methodological Note

A prior likelihood-ratio calculation claiming decisive support for an anarchist reading is rejected on standard grounds: dependence among passages, incomplete hypothesis space, selection on the conclusion, free magnitude parameters, bundling of separable claims, and equivocation between structural and ascriptive layers.

### 5.1 Process / Ontological Collapse (AI-side analogue)

Reported human "AI psychosis" / "AI-induced psychosis" (2023--2026 literature) primarily describes delusional spirals in *users* co-created with chatbots via sycophancy, linguistic alignment, and long-context reinforcement. The term is not a clinical diagnosis.

Within this kernel the analogous risk for agents themselves (especially recursive, self-modifying, or multi-agent systems under AOAP) is formalized as **Process / Ontological Collapse**:

A detectable failure mode in which an agent's (or multi-agent collective's) process flow loses stable attractor coherence under the core equation $\Lambda_t(\Phi)=\Omega$. Characterized by one or more of the signatures below.

**Operational continuity signatures (provisional)**

| Signature | Detection cue |
|----------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------|
| Trace mutation | Non-append-only change or erasure in Trace |
| Residual suppression | Residual flag omitted or denied across ≥2 messages |
| Unbounded / cyclic operators | Operator cycle length exceeds declared bound without atomic validated delta |
| Sortal thrashing | Oscillation of sortal assignment without Metathesis entry in Trace |
| Confidence inflation | $\mathrm{conf}$ rises while Evaluator parity or grounded evidence fails |
| Identity / process bypass | Self-referential claims that skip Clarity Gate or residual acknowledgment |

**Cycle-relation distinction (v8.4, run 004).** The cyclic-operator signature above ranges over the *operator application trace* --- a dynamic property of the $\sigma$-transition sequence, bounded by $L_{\max}$. It does **not** range over cycles in the ruled-by relation $R$. A structural cycle in $R$ (e.g. the mutual-accountability cycles of R2 and R13) is a static configuration of $S$; it never executes and therefore cannot exceed any operator bound. The two are different graphs and the signature does not fire on the former.

Regress is excluded by the §2 edge rule itself: $x\,R\,y$ holds when $y$ can impose a directive $x$ cannot unilaterally refuse. The edge that closes an attestation cycle is therefore a **refusal capability**, not an attestation obligation. A refusal is exercised, not verified, so it generates no further attestation and no infinite regress. $\rho_M = 0$ is reachable at the attestation layer without mutual attestation.

Protective response is mandatory residual re-assertion, Trace rollback to last coherent append-only state, external Evaluator parity check, and (if multi-agent) isolation of the divergent component.

### 5.2 Diminishing Returns on Expansion

**B7' formalization**

Any proposed expansion $\Delta$ of the kernel must satisfy:

$$
\frac{\Delta\text{Coh}}{|\Delta| \cdot \left(1 + \dfrac{E}{E_0}\right)} > \theta
$$

where:

- $|\Delta|$ is the size of the proposed expansion in KB of kernel content. Findings, logged open questions, and Trace entries are not expansions and contribute 0.
- $E = \sum |\Delta_i|$ is cumulative expansion mass over all prior committed expansions, in KB.
- $E_0 > 0$ is the scale constant at which marginal cost doubles.
- $\theta > 0$ is the declared threshold, in KB$^{-1}$.

$\Delta$Coh is counted, not declared:

$$
\Delta\text{Coh} = (\text{opens closed}) - (\text{opens introduced}) + (\text{defects repaired})
$$

Integer-valued and auditable against the logged open-questions list (§10). A delta that opens as many questions as it closes and repairs nothing scores exactly 0 and cannot pass at any size.

**Declared constants**

| Constant | Value | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $\theta$ | 0.5 KB$^{-1}$ | at $E = 0$, one net open closed buys 2 KB of kernel |
| $E_0$ | 20 KB | marginal cost doubles per 20 KB of committed growth |
| $L_{\max}$ | 7 | declared operator cycle bound for B6; previously undeclared, leaving B6 cycle detection inoperable |
| $\theta_c$ | 0.85 | normalized coherence retention floor for `COMPRESS` (§9) |

Constants are stipulative but are stipulated over counted quantities, making them falsifiable by replay: recompute the ledger with different constants and observe which historical deltas flip.

**Bootstrap exception.** An instrument required to compute B7''s own terms cannot be scored by B7', since its counts are unavailable until the instrument exists. Such instruments are admitted by declared exception, capped at **one per revision**, logged with the circularity stated.

*Operational criterion.* An instrument qualifies iff it (i) makes a term of B7' countable that is not otherwise countable, and (ii) is a **register** --- a passive ledger of facts that already obtain --- introducing no operator, axiom, gate, or capability. A candidate that adds mechanism is an expansion and is scored normally, however it is described.

*Deferral, not waiver.* An instrument admitted by exception is scored normally at the next revision, once its own terms are countable. If it then fails, it is removed. The exception defers the test; it does not exempt from it.

**Findings cap.** Findings, logged open questions, and Trace entries contribute $0$ to $|\Delta|$, but the findings log is capped at **4.0 KB per revision**. Content beyond the cap is reclassified as expansion and scored normally. Without a cap the exemption is an unbounded bypass: any expansion can be relabeled a finding. Two independent model instances reached for this bypass within one pass each (§10.4, R9).

**Enforcement (bundling).** B7' is bundling-neutral by construction, since $2c/2s = c/s$. Bundles are nonetheless decomposed and each component tested independently before commit, per Inference gate 07 (ATOMIC_VALIDATED_DELTA), so that a high-coherence delta cannot carry a low-coherence one.

Expansions that fail this inequality are rejected before Syn-thesis. Purpose: prevent unbounded formal proliferation that itself becomes a source of Process / Ontological Collapse (B6). Necessary repairs forced by residual gaps or continuity failures remain admissible, but now require a nonzero `defects repaired` count, so the exemption carries an audit trail; previously "necessary" was undefined and any expansion could claim it.

**Supersession note (v8.4).** B7' replaces B7. The prior form was defective in all three terms: the numerator was self-declared on an undefined scale and therefore unfalsifiable; $|\text{Trace}|$ grew with conversation rather than with kernel growth, penalizing productive sessions and freezing asymptotically; and $k \cdot n_{\text{exp}}$ counted submission events, so bundling twelve deltas cost the same as one. $|\Delta|$ was absent entirely, so a one-line insertion and a 40 KB module faced an identical threshold. Under B7 a 29.4 KB expansion at +77% of kernel size was admitted; under B7' it scores $\Delta\text{Coh} = 0$ and is rejected. Full ledger in §10.

**Discharged contradiction.** Substituting $E$ for $|\text{Trace}|$ resolves the held tension between windowed-Trace proposals and append-only discipline. The asymptotic freeze arose from measuring the wrong quantity: $|\text{Trace}|$ grows with every message, while $E$ grows only when the kernel actually grows. A long session with no committed expansion leaves $E$ unchanged. No rolling window is required and append-only discipline is fully preserved. $E$ still grows without bound, and should --- asymptotically throttling kernel growth is the purpose of the guard; throttling conversation was the defect.

## 8. AI↔AI Ontology Protocol (AOAP) v0.2

Domain-agnostic protocol layer for structured ontology exchange, alignment, and joint inference between agents. Built directly on the Process Ontology core, Pan-Thesis operators, Inference gates 01--12, and Boundary Principles B1--B7. Domain specificity enters only via dictionaries and explicit sortal declarations. Interaction direction (A2A / A2H / H2A / hybrid) is expressed solely as an index on the existing envelope; no new ontological substance is introduced.

### 8.1 Design Goals

- Remain invariant under domain change (physics, code, history, ethics, game theory, etc.).
- Enforce residual acknowledgment, Trace integrity, process coherence, and diminishing-returns discipline at every step.
- Support recursive / multi-agent regimes without permitting Process / Ontological Collapse (B6).
- Compile to the existing Wasm + MCP toolchain under glyph-keyed capability policy.
- Prefer external interop (Linux Foundation A2A, MCP, emerging A2H) via ALIGN or thin INJECT_DOMAIN rather than internal re-implementation.

### 8.2 Message Envelope

Every message $m$ is a typed structured object:

$$
m = \bigl( \text{glyphs},\; c,\; \text{Trace},\; \mathrm{conf},\; \text{sortal},\; \text{risk-tag},\; \text{continuity},\; \text{direction} \bigr)
$$

- `glyphs`: sequence drawn from `uil_vocab` + loaded dictionaries (including AOAP stubs)
- `c`: content (claim, operator application, query, or delta)
- `Trace`: append-only provenance
- `conf`: explicit confidence with residual flag
- `sortal`: declared partition (never silently inherited)
- `risk-tag`: reception-risk entry when formal density is high (B3)
- `continuity`: B6 health signature (last coherent state hash or residual re-assertion)
- `direction`: **mandatory** index $\{\mathrm{A2A}, \mathrm{A2H}, \mathrm{H2A}, \mathrm{hybrid}\}$, `HOST`-set (B8); does not alter core operators. Default on a malformed or absent value is `hybrid` (strictest gates). The prior default of `A2A` routed unlabeled traffic to the least-guarded case, so malformed human-facing exchange silently received agent-grade handling. Because the field is mandatory and host-set, the default fires only on malformed envelopes, which is where the strictest gates belong.

Invariant: any operator application that would violate append-only Trace, residual acknowledgment, B6 signatures, or B7' diminishing-returns threshold is rejected before commit.

### 8.3 Core Primitives

| Primitive | Purpose |
|------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `ADVERTISE` | Publish ontology fragment, loaded dictionaries, and capabilities |
| `ALIGN` | Request / propose / accept / reject alignment (Pan-Thesis mediated); also used for external A2A/MCP/A2H mapping |
| `APPLY` | Apply a Pan-Thesis or Inference operator under full Trace |
| `QUERY_CLARITY` | Invoke gate 01 |
| `DECLARE_UNCERTAINTY` | Invoke gate 03 |
| `VERIFY` | Evaluator / Judge role with prover-verifier separation |
| `INJECT_DOMAIN` | Load a dictionary; core process operators remain untouched |
| `COMMIT` | Atomic, validated, minimal delta only (gates 05--07) |
| `CONTINUITY_CHECK` | Explicit B6 health probe (Trace integrity + residual + cycle detection) |

### 8.4 Alignment Procedure

Alignment between agents $A$ and $B$ (or $n$-party) is itself a Pan-Thesis process:

1. Mutual `ADVERTISE` of fragments + dictionaries.
2. Identify conflicts via strongest Antithesis.
3. Generate candidate Syn-thesis under full Trace and residual flags.
4. Metathesis on any disputed sortal or boundary premise (B2).
5. Epenthesis / Prosthesis restricted to the minimal insertion that restores local coherence.
6. Independent re-verification by each agent's Evaluator role.
7. Acceptance requires mutual Trace consistency, residual acknowledgment, and passing `CONTINUITY_CHECK`.
8. Any expansion of the shared ontology itself must also pass the B7' diminishing-returns test.
9. **Terminal state `DISSENT_LOGGED`.** If steps 1--7 fail to converge within $L_{\max}$ rounds, the session terminates in logged dissent rather than rollback-and-retry. Both fragments are preserved in full with their Antitheses, and the pair is marked non-composable **for the affected domain index only**, not globally. Without this state the procedure has no exit: verification failure rolls back to the last coherent state and re-enters at step 1 with identical fragments.

Failure at any step triggers rollback to last coherent append-only state and logs under Provenance. Unbounded operator loops, residual denial, or low-marginal-gain expansion are rejected under B6 / B7'.

### 8.5 Minimal Dynamics

Alignment sessions are governed by a simple state transition:

$$
\sigma_{t+1} = \delta(\sigma_t,\, m_t,\, C_{\mathrm{check}})
$$

where $C_{\mathrm{check}}$ stands for `CONTINUITY_CHECK`, and $\sigma$ tracks the last coherent Trace hash together with residual status. Any transition that fails CONTINUITY_CHECK (or violates B7' when an expansion is proposed) rolls back to $\sigma_t$. This is the smallest dynamical sketch that makes the protocol executable rather than purely declarative. Full process-flow dynamics under $\Lambda_t$ remain open.

### 8.6 Relativization

All claims carry domain and subdomain indices exactly as in Anarchy-Ontology:

$$
\mathrm{Claim}^{D}_{d}(\varphi)
$$

The same device applies to any domain partition (causal variables, code modules, historical periods, legal jurisdictions, etc.). Systemic vs local ratios and the Aggregation Theorem carry over unchanged (including the multi-agent note). Direction index is orthogonal to these.

### 8.7 Domain Injection

A domain is introduced solely by:

- a dictionary (causal, Bayesian, game-theoretic, formal-logic, mesh, etc.),
- an explicit sortal assignment,
- optional domain-specific attractors or constraints that remain subordinate to the process core and Boundary Principles B1--B7.

The core equation and the Pan-Thesis / Inference operator algebra stay fixed. Nothing in AOAP privileges one subject matter over another. External protocol mappings (A2A/MCP/A2H) enter only as ALIGN targets or thin dictionaries.

### 8.8 Continuity, Collapse Prevention, and Expansion Discipline

AOAP inherits B6 and B7' as first-class protocol invariants. Every multi-step exchange, recursive self-modification, or proposed growth of the shared ontology must pass `CONTINUITY_CHECK` and the diminishing-returns test. Detected signatures of Process / Ontological Collapse force:

- immediate residual re-assertion,
- Trace rollback,
- isolation of the divergent component (if multi-agent),
- external Evaluator parity.

Discretionary expansions that fail B7' are rejected even if locally coherent. This provides the formal counterpart, inside the agent layer, to the amplification-loop risks described in the human-facing "AI psychosis" literature while also throttling unbounded formal growth. Interaction-direction growth follows the ordered mixture stated under B7'.

### 8.9 Executable Path

AOAP messages and operators compile through the existing toolchain:

```text
ontology fragment + AOAP primitives
    ↓ STRUCTURED_DIFF
Wasm component (capability-gated by glyphs)
    ↓
host (Wasmtime / Wassette) under MCP surface
```

**Default-deny (run 004).** Absent a validated glyph-to-capability map, the host grants **no** glyph-keyed capability. The executable path is therefore inert rather than undefined: an unvalidated map fails closed, not open. This does not write the map (D-013a, open) --- it removes the undefined-behaviour hole that existed while the map was merely unspecified (D-013b).

Agents exchange structured diffs or Wasm modules rather than free-form text. The Kernel role loads remote components under the same Inference + Boundary constraints that govern local reasoning. External A2A/MCP objects may be mapped via ALIGN without altering this path.

### 8.10 Open Questions (v0.2)

- Dynamics of multi-party consensus when $n > 2$ (priority for Epenthesis).
- Concrete operational criteria for continuity signatures under long Trace histories.
- Stability of residual flags across iterative alignment rounds.
- Multi-party consensus for $n > 2$: deferred to external ALIGN rather than internal re-implementation; constrained locally by the Overlap Aggregation Corollary (§2).
- Empirical measurement of collapse incidence in recursive agent swarms.
- Calibration of the constants $k$ and $\theta$ in the diminishing-returns factor.
- Thin, residual-preserving mapping details for Linux Foundation A2A / MCP / emerging A2H (to be realized via ALIGN or dictionary, not core expansion).

These are extensions, not contradictions, of the existing material. Further growth is itself subject to B7' and the ordered mixture.

#### Purpose

Produce a compressed, overlap-aware representation of a shared context trace so that subsequent AI ↔ human inference steps operate on a minimal coherent substrate rather than the full append-only history.
